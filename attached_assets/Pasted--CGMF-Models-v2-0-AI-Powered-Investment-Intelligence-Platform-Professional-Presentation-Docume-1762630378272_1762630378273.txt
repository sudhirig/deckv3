##### Risk Assessment with AI
**Advanced Metrics Explained:**
- **Sharpe Ratio**: Return per unit of risk (higher is better) - "Are you getting paid enough for the risk you're taking?"
- **Sortino Ratio**: Like Sharpe but focuses only on downside risk - "How well does the fund protect during bad times?"
- **Calmar Ratio**: Return compared to maximum drawdown - "How much did you earn vs worst-case loss?"
- **Value at Risk (VaR)**: Maximum expected loss with 95% confidence - "In a bad month, you won't lose more than X%"
- **Maximum Drawdown**: Worst peak-to-trough decline - "The biggest historical loss was X%"

**User Benefit:** You understand not just returns, but the risk you're taking to achieve them. Fund A with 18% returns and 25% volatility might be worse than Fund B with 16% returns and 10% volatility.
