## ⚠️ WHAT NOT TO DO

### **Avoid These Pitfalls:**

1. **DON'T claim traction you don't have**
   - No "live clients" 
   - No "AUM under management"
   - No revenue claims

2. **DON'T oversell the AI**
   - Be specific about what it does TODAY
   - Separate current prototype from future vision
   - No "AGI" or unrealistic claims

3. **DON'T hide the B2B2C strategy**
   - It's your credible GTM, not a weakness
   - The CPA channel reduces CAC and risk
   - Frame as "Hybrid" not "pivot"

4. **DON'T make Arta the enemy**
   - They validate the market
   - Your B2B2C channel is differentiated
   - Position as "different approach" not "better"

5. **DON'T forget the India moat**
   - It's your only true blue ocean
   - Vora partnership is exclusive
   - This is Phase 1 for a reason
