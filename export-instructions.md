## How to Export as PDF

### Step 1: Navigate to the Presentation
Open your presentation in the browser (it should already be running).

### Step 2: Open Print Dialog
- **Windows/Linux:** Press `Ctrl + P`
- **Mac:** Press `Cmd + P`

### Step 3: Configure Print Settings
1. **Destination:** Select "Save as PDF"
2. **Layout:** Landscape
3. **Margins:** Default or Custom (0.5 inches recommended)
4. **Background graphics:** Enable (important for colors and gradients)
5. **Scale:** 100% (default)

### Step 4: Save the PDF
1. Click "Save" or "Print"
2. Use the recommended filename above: `AI-Digital-Family-Office-Deck_2025-11-05.pdf`
3. Choose your desired save location
