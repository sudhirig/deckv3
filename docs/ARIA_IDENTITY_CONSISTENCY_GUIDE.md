## ✅ ALWAYS USE THESE TERMS

### **Primary Descriptors:**
- **ARIA** (never "the platform" alone)
- **AI Family Office CEO** (official title)
- **She/Her** (never "it")
- **Orchestrates** (primary verb)
- **Commands** (shows authority)
- **5 Specialized Teams** (not agents)

### **Supporting Language:**
- "Your wealth's AI CEO"
- "24/7 intelligence"
- "Virtuoso of wealth management"
- "Conductor of your financial symphony"
- "Leader of specialized teams"
- "Architect of your prosperity"

### **Capability Descriptions:**
- "ARIA analyzes..." (not "the system analyzes")
- "She decides..." (not "it decides")
- "ARIA orchestrates..." (not "manages")
- "She protects..." (personified action)
- "ARIA commands her teams..." (leadership)

## ❌ NEVER USE THESE TERMS

### **Forbidden Words:**
| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|--------------|----------------|
| Chatbot | AI Intelligence |
| Bot | Intelligence |
| Swarm | Orchestra |
| Agents (alone) | Specialized Teams |
| It/Its | She/Her |
| The Platform (alone) | ARIA Platform |
| System | ARIA |
| Tool | Intelligence/CEO |
| Assistant | CEO/Leader |
| Manages | Orchestrates |

### **Problematic Phrases to Avoid:**
- ❌ "ARIA is a platform that..."
- ❌ "The system uses agents to..."
- ❌ "It analyzes your portfolio..."
- ❌ "Our bot can help you..."
- ❌ "The swarm of agents..."
- ❌ "ARIA manages your wealth..."
