## 💡 Why This Structure Works

### **Clear Progression:**
1. **Personal** (inequality) →
2. **Geographic** (trapped portfolio) →
3. **Barriers** (three walls) →
4. **Urgency** (18-month window) →
5. **Scale** (AI necessity) →
6. **Gap** ($124T opportunity) →
7. **Failure** (current solutions don't work)

### **Both Dimensions Covered:**
- **ACCESS:** Three Walls, Geographic Trap
- **INTELLIGENCE:** Scale Only AI Can Handle
- **BOTH:** Dual Gap + Why Solutions Fail

### **Strong Ending:**
Slide 7 reinforces that you need BOTH access AND intelligence, setting up perfectly for ARIA as the solution in Act 2.
