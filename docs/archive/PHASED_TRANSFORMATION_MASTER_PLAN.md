## 🚨 RISK MITIGATION STRATEGY

### **For Each Phase:**
1. **Backup Current State** - Before any changes
2. **Create Branch/Version** - For rollback ability
3. **Test Incrementally** - Don't make all changes at once
4. **Get Approval** - From key stakeholders
5. **Document Changes** - What and why

### **Red Flags to Watch:**
- Story becomes unclear → Rollback and reassess
- Technical details lost → Move to appendix, don't delete
- Team disagreement → Pause and align
- Investor confusion → Simplify further
- Time exceeds 20 minutes → Cut more aggressively
